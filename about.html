About Us

Orchid Buds School is a vibrant early education center in Kanpur. We offer classes from Playgroup to Class 4 in a fun, engaging, and safe environment. Facilities include:

  • Fully air-conditioned classrooms
  • Interactive smart boards
  • Outdoor play area
  • Creative arts and crafts
  • Nutritious meals
